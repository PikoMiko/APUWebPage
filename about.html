About The Championship

Compete. Connect. Celebrate.

The APU Sports Championship is an annual inter-faculty competition organized to promote health, teamwork, and sportsmanship among students of Asia Pacific University of Technology & Innovation. Now in its fourth edition, the championship has grown into one of APU's most anticipated student events.

Background

The APU Sports Championship was first organized in 2023 as a small interfaculty football competition with fewer than 50 participants. Recognizing the positive impact it had on student engagement and campus spirit, the organizing committee expanded the event in subsequent years to include Basketball and Swimming. By 2026, the championship has grown to welcome over 200 registered participants and draws spectators from across all faculties. The event is entirely student-led, giving participants the opportunity to develop leadership, event management, and communication skills alongside their athletic pursuits.

Objectives

  • 🏃 Promote Active Lifestyles — Encourage APU students to participate in regular physical activity and maintain a healthy lifestyle through organized sport.
  • 🤝 Foster Teamwork & Collaboration — Build strong bonds between students from different programs and faculties through team-based competition.
  • 🏫 Strengthen Faculty Spirit — Provide a platform for students to represent their faculty with pride and develop a sense of belonging within the APU community.
  • 🌟 Recognise Talent — Identify and celebrate sporting talent among the student population through fair and well-organized competitions.
  • 📋 Develop Leadership Skills — Offer students leadership opportunities through volunteer and committee roles in event planning and management.

Target Audience & Scope

The championship is open to all currently enrolled APU students. Participation is entirely free of charge, with no prior competitive experience required.

🎓 Participants

Full-time APU students from any faculty. Teams must consist of registered students only. Student ID required at check-in.

👀 Spectators

All APU students, staff, and invited guests are welcome to watch the matches and support their faculty teams.

🙋 Volunteers

Students who wish to contribute by assisting with event setup, registration, media coverage, or refereeing.

🏢 Sponsors

Organizations and companies who support the event through funding, equipment, or promotional partnership.

Past Editions

2023 — Inaugural Edition

Launched as a single-sport football competition with 8 teams and 40 participants. School of Technology claimed the first-ever championship title.

2024 — Second Edition

Basketball was introduced as the second sport. Participation grew to over 100 students. School of Business emerged as the overall champion.

2025 — Third Edition

Swimming was added, completing the current three-sport format. Over 150 students participated. School of Engineering won the most gold medals across all events.

2026 — Fourth Edition (Current)

The largest edition yet with over 200 registered participants, 5 faculty teams, and the introduction of a volunteer programme and formal sponsorship partnerships.
