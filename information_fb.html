Event Information

Welcome to the APU Sports Day Football tournament!

This tournament will be played in a 4v4 format.

Teams will compete in a single-elimination format across three rounds:

  • Quarterfinals
  • Semi-finals
  • Grand Finals
  • Additional Matches:
  • 3rd Place Match
  • Showmatch: APU Football Team vs Winner of this tournament

Team Information

Each team must have 4 players registered for the tournament.

Teams are encouraged to bring their own uniforms, but these will also be provided at the venue.

Matches will be played on a futsal with standard futsal rules. Each match will consist of two 10-minute halves, with a 5-minute halftime break. In the event of a tie, a penalty shootout will be played.
