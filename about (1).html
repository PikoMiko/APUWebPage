About The Event

The APU Sports Championship is an annual event organized to promote health, teamwork, and sportsmanship among students from various faculties. Our mission is to bring students together through competitive and fun sports activities.

The event is held over 3 days at APU campus in Bukit Jalil, Kuala Lumpur. It is open to all currently enrolled APU students and is completely free to participate. Now in its 4th edition, the championship has become one of the most anticipated student events of the year.

Our Objectives

  • Encourage students to adopt an active and healthy lifestyle through sport.
  • Build teamwork and collaboration between students from different faculties.
  • Celebrate and recognise sporting talent within the APU student community.
  • Strengthen faculty spirit and a sense of belonging on campus.
  • Provide students with leadership opportunities through volunteer and committee roles.

Past Editions

  • 2023 — Inaugural Edition
    Launched as a football-only competition with 8 teams and around 40 participants. School of Technology won the first ever championship title.
  • 2024 — Second Edition
    Basketball was introduced as the second sport. Participation grew to over 100 students across two events.
  • 2025 — Third Edition
    Swimming was added to complete the three-sport format. Over 150 students took part and School of Engineering topped the medal tally.
  • 2026 — Fourth Edition (Current)
    The biggest edition yet with over 200 registered participants, a formal volunteer programme, and new sponsorship partnerships.
