Event Information

Welcome to the APU Sports Day Swimming tournament!

This tournament will have 4 different races. Team points are won through winning races.

Teams will compete in these races:

  • 50m Freestyle
  • 100m Freestyle
  • 50m Backstroke
  • 100m Backstroke

Team Information

Each team will have a maximum of 4 players registered for the tournament.

Teams are encouraged to bring their own uniforms, but these will also be provided at the venue.
